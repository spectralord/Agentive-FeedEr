Seven flat links become four destinations

The nav was never redesigned as the app grew from 3 screens to 12. Seven links plus the brand currently sit in one flex row inside max-w-xl — that overflows a phone, and Knowledge Base would make eight. They also aren't peers: they're consume, retrieve, grow, and ops. Tap the bottom bar.

Two hub tabs each hold three sub-views behind a segmented control. New surfaces go into a hub, not onto the top bar — so this stops being a problem that returns every epic.

What moved and why

Overview → Archive, inside Library. Its SOTA half is superseded by Skill Guides (see the note below); its History half is retrieval, which Guides don't do — so it survives, and it's where the missing search belongs.

Saved + Experience were never top-level concerns — they're things you go looking for, which is what a Library is.

Admin is an ops surface in a single-user app. A gear, not a tab.

The one real tension

A persistent bottom bar eats ~56px of every full-screen snap card, and it collides with the floating ReelActions bar. Fix: auto-hide on scroll-down, reveal on scroll-up — the pattern people already know from Instagram and Twitter — with the action bar riding above it when shown. Switch to the Feed tab to see the note about this.

Today as the root. Worth considering separately: / is currently the Feed, but the daily ritual is Today. Making Today the landing route matches how the product is actually used — open it, get your three things, finish. The Feed already has a "To the full feed" path from Today's ending card.
