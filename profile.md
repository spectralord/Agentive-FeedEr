## Title quality
An item whose title carries no information on its own — a bare version string like
`v2.1.210`, or "Weekly update" — is worth less to me than its content alone suggests,
because I cannot tell from the feed whether it is worth opening. Judge such items on
their actual content, but do not let a substantive changelog behind a meaningless title
score as highly as the same substance behind a descriptive one.
